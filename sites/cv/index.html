Personal statement

I am an occupational and organisational psychologist working as a Talent Scientist at Canonical, where I apply psychometric assessment, behavioural science, and quantitative analysis to selection at scale across a fully distributed, global organisation.

My training is a research training: a First-Class MSc in Occupational and Organisational Psychology (University of Surrey) and a First-Class BSc in Forensic Psychology (Coventry University), alongside three years as a research assistant on a doctoral project running high-complexity experimental work. My methodological interests sit in the measurement of individual differences — psychometric scale development, factor analysis, and regression modelling — and in how evidence-based selection can be made both more accurate and fairer.
